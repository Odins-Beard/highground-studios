GYM PRO

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 14 October 2026

OVERVIEW

Gym Pro is a workout tracking application developed by Highground Studios.

We believe your workout information should remain yours. Gym Pro is designed to store your training information on your device without requiring an account or cloud storage.

INFORMATION STORED

Gym Pro stores workout programs, exercises, sets, weights, repetitions, activity durations and related preferences locally on your device.

This information is used to provide the workout tracking functionality of the app.

DATA COLLECTION & SHARING

Highground Studios does not operate user accounts or collect your workout records through its own servers.

Gym Pro does not currently include third-party advertising or analytics services.

DATA STORAGE & DELETION

Your workout information is stored locally on your device.

You can remove the app's locally stored information by clearing its app data through your device settings or uninstalling Gym Pro.

ADVERTISING

Gym Pro currently displays a promotional placeholder for another Highground Studios project.

If third-party advertising is introduced in a future version, this policy will be updated to explain any relevant data collection or sharing.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this privacy policy as Gym Pro develops. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

CONTACT & INFORMATION

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact Highground Studios at info@highgroundstudios.net
