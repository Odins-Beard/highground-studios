POOL SHARK

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 5 November 2026

OVERVIEW

Pool Shark is a pool match and session tracking application developed by Highground Studios.

This policy explains the information used to provide the app's functionality and how you can manage it.

INFORMATION STORED

The app uses information such as player names, venues, match results, session history and game preferences to provide its features.

Some of this information may be retained on your device between sessions.

DATA COLLECTION & SHARING

The app's core features use player and match records. Any additional data collected or shared by integrated services must be described here before publication.

DATA STORAGE & DELETION

You can clear locally stored app information through your device settings or by uninstalling the app. Information shared or stored outside your device may require separate deletion.

ADVERTISING & EXTERNAL SERVICES

Pool Shark allows you to export and import session history between devices. Exported records may include player names, venues and session results. You choose whether to export and share this information; people you send it to may be able to access those records.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this privacy policy as Pool Shark develops. Changes will be published here with an updated revision date.

CONTACT & INFORMATION

For questions about this policy, contact info@highgroundstudios.net.
