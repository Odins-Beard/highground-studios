TELEBOX

ANDROID IPTV PLAYER · V0.4.3

TeleBox is an IPTV player for Android phones, tablets and Android TV devices. Connect using your Xtream Codes provider details to browse live TV channels, films and TV series. Stream your content or download films and episodes to watch offline.

WHAT TELEBOX DOES

Browse Your Content Browse films, series and live TV using List, Image and Grid views.
Find & Organise Search by title, browse categories, sort your catalogue, save favourites and mark titles as watched.
Offline Viewing Download films and individual TV episodes to watch without an internet connection.
Playback Controls Adjust streaming buffer settings and playback tracks where supported.
Your IPTV Provider TeleBox requires your own compatible Xtream Codes IPTV account. It does not provide channels, subscriptions or streaming content.

WHAT'S NEW IN V0.4.3

Android TV Playback Focus Playback controls now remain mounted when hidden. Press OK to reveal them without interrupting playback, with Play/Pause retained as the focus target.
Pause & Exit Behaviour Controls stay visible while paused. Pressing Back while paused opens the exit confirmation instead of hiding the controls.
TV Player Layout Adjusted the placement of playback toolbars and Exit, and restored brighter controls for landscape and Android TV while retaining softer portrait controls.
Remote Navigation Refinements Updated preferred focus on film and series details, improved Last Watched episode focus, and made downloaded artwork selectable using the TV remote.
Screen Spacing & Refresh Status Added horizontal padding on Android TV screens and kept the Connected status displayed during catalogue refreshes.
