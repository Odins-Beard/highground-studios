TELEBOX

ANDROID IPTV PLAYER · V0.4.12

TeleBox is an IPTV player for Android phones, tablets and Android TV devices. Connect using your Xtream Codes provider details to browse live TV channels, films and TV series. Stream your content or download films and episodes to watch offline.

WHAT TELEBOX DOES

Browse Your Content Browse films, series and live TV using List, Image and Grid views.
Find & Organise Search by title, browse categories, sort your catalogue, save favourites and mark titles as watched.
Offline Viewing Download films and individual TV episodes to watch without an internet connection.
Playback Controls Adjust streaming buffer settings and playback tracks where supported.
Your IPTV Provider TeleBox requires your own compatible Xtream Codes IPTV account. It does not provide channels, subscriptions or streaming content.

WHAT'S NEW IN V0.4.12

Consistent Phone & Tablet Navigation Refined the catalogue toolbar for phones and tablets, with categories displayed below the controls in portrait and between the controls in landscape.
Android TV Search & Categories Restored Search to the Android TV toolbar, improved control grouping and separators, and made the selected category visible in its dropdown.
Search & View Controls Added a responsive search field beneath the toolbar, improved dropdown spacing and view labels, and updated Search highlighting to match other controls.
Downloads Grid Improvements Aligned Downloads grid tile sizing with Films and Series and corrected grid column handling when the screen rotates.
Playback & Navigation Refinements Improved Android TV playback focus and episode navigation, and added a setting for playback control auto-hide timing.
