TELEBOX

ANDROID APP · V0.3.56

TeleBox is a simple IPTV player for Android. Connect using your Xtream Codes provider details to browse live TV channels, films and TV series, and play your content in one place.

WHAT TELEBOX DOES

Browse films, series and live TV with List, Image and Grid views.

Find titles using search, categories and sorting. Save favourites, mark titles as watched and return to programmes you've started.

TeleBox requires your own compatible IPTV provider account. It does not include TV channels or streaming content.
