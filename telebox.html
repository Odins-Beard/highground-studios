TELEBOX

ANDROID IPTV PLAYER · V0.3.56

TeleBox is an IPTV player for Android phones, tablets and Android TV devices. Connect using your Xtream Codes provider details to browse live TV channels, films and TV series. Stream your content or download films and episodes to watch offline.

WHAT TELEBOX DOES

Browse Your Content Browse films, series and live TV using List, Image and Grid views.
Find & Organise Search by title, browse categories, sort your catalogue, save favourites and mark titles as watched.
Offline Viewing Download films and individual TV episodes to watch without an internet connection.
Playback Controls Adjust streaming buffer settings and playback tracks where supported.
Your IPTV Provider TeleBox requires your own compatible Xtream Codes IPTV account. It does not provide channels, subscriptions or streaming content.

WHAT'S NEW IN V0.3.56

Offline Downloads Download films and individual TV series episodes for offline viewing.
Download Manager Queue multiple downloads, pause and resume transfers, monitor progress and manage completed files.
Android TV Navigation Improved navigation controls for Android TV devices and tablets.
Responsive Layout Refined catalogue navigation in portrait and landscape orientations.
