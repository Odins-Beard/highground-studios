TELEBOX

ANDROID IPTV PLAYER · V0.3.56

TeleBox is an IPTV player for Android phones, tablets and Android TV devices. Connect using your Xtream Codes provider details to browse live TV channels, films and TV series. Stream your content or download films and episodes to watch offline.

WHAT TELEBOX DOES

Browse films, series and live TV with List, Image and Grid views.

Find titles using search, categories and sorting. Save favourites, mark titles as watched and return to programmes you've started.

Download films and individual TV episodes for offline viewing. Manage downloads with queueing, pause and resume controls, and progress tracking.

Adjust streaming buffer settings and playback tracks where supported.

TeleBox requires your own compatible IPTV provider account using Xtream Codes. It does not provide TV channels, subscriptions or streaming content.
