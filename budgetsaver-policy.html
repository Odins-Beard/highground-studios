BUDGET SAVER

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 5 November 2026

OVERVIEW

Budget Saver is a budget planning and expense tracking application developed by Highground Studios.

This policy explains the information used to provide the app's functionality and how you can manage it.

INFORMATION STORED

The app uses information such as budgets, spending entries, commitments, adjustments and related preferences to provide its features.

Some of this information may be retained on your device between sessions.

DATA COLLECTION & SHARING

The app's core features use budget and spending information. Any additional data collected or shared by integrated services must be described here before publication.

DATA STORAGE & DELETION

You can clear locally stored app information through your device settings or by uninstalling the app. Information shared or stored outside your device may require separate deletion.

ADVERTISING & EXTERNAL SERVICES

Budget Saver may display a promotional banner or advertising. The data practices of any third-party advertising services must be confirmed before publication.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this privacy policy as Budget Saver develops. Changes will be published here with an updated revision date.

CONTACT & INFORMATION

For questions about this policy, contact info@highgroundstudios.net.
