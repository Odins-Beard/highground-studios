ANDROID INSTALLATION

Welcome to our Android installation page, and thank you for your interest in our apps.

Our Android apps are currently available through Google Play's early-access testing process. There are a few steps involved, thanks to Google's requirements, but we've made things as straightforward as possible.

First, join our Google Group. Next, opt in to the app you'd like to try using its own tester link. Finally, install it through Google Play. You only need to join the Google Group once, even if you want to install several apps.
